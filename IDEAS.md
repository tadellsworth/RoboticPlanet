# Ideas waiting their turn

Notes on features we've talked about but not built yet. (The nuclear race and the desktop RTS controls started
here and are now in the game.)

## Road maintenance

Make the Roadworks a maintenance station you place strategically.

- **Choose which roads to pave:** a Roadworks only paves and maintains the roads you mark for it, set from the
  Roadworks panel or from the Paths menu, instead of every road joined to it.
- **Wear:** paved roads wear down with traffic. The busier a road, the faster it wears; worn asphalt slows bots, and a
  road worn right through drops back to plain concrete until it's repaved.
- **Repaving:** the Paver goes back to worn tiles on its marked roads and resurfaces them with Asphalt.

## Biomes

A new-game choice of planet: the current dusty red **Mars-like** world stays the default, plus a **jungle** world and a
**polar** world, each with its own ground colours and its own decorative plant life. Plants are scenery only: they
never block bots, and a building, road or construction site simply clears the plants on its tiles.
