# Ideas waiting their turn

Notes on features we've talked about but not built yet.

## Optional nuclear setting (uranium race)

An optional new-game setting, off by default.

- **Uranium:** a small amount sprinkled across the map, scarce enough that finding enough is the challenge.
- **Two new buildings:**
  - **Nuclear Reactor** (or nuclear refinery): turns mined uranium into fissile material.
  - **Nuclear Missile Bay**: builds and launches a nuclear bomb.
- **The bomb** obliterates a whole region, not just a building and its neighbours.
- **The game it creates:** a race to find and refine enough uranium to build a bomb before the opponent does.
- **Art:** the nuclear missile silo, nuclear missile and nuclear refinery sprites are already made in Claude Design.
  Bring them in when this feature is built.
- **Still to decide:** how to mine uranium (its own mine building, or the Ore Mine), how much a bomb needs, the blast
  radius and what it does to land (destroy buildings, bots and roads; maybe leave a scorched area for a while),
  whether there's any defence or warning (a countdown the target can see?), and how the rival AI plays the race.

## Desktop mode as a traditional RTS

Make the desktop version play more like a traditional RTS while keeping touch play simple on phones.

- A full set of keyboard hotkeys: build-menu shortcuts per building, control groups for warbots, select-all-army,
  jump to the last alert, cycle through idle bots or buildings, quick game-speed and pause controls.
- Mouse: drag-box selection of warbots, right-click to move or attack, edge-of-screen scrolling, hover tooltips.
- Mobile stays tap-first: none of this replaces the touch controls.
