# Terrafall Campaign Outline

Main campaign design, 15 missions in three acts (drafted 2026-11-16). Companion to `lore/terrafall-lore-bible.md`. The editable working copy is the Claude Doc "Terrafall Campaign Outline".

Fifteen missions in three acts follow the last reserve Command Base on COBALT's Carrier, from its landing in 2165 to the moment it learns no further instructions will follow. Underneath the war, the Murmur grow on every battle.

## Act I: Landfall

The colony learns to build. The war stays at the edges, and the Murmur are only a sound in the ground.

### 1. Terrafall

- **Map:** The Rust (red desert). No rivals.
- **Objectives:** Land the Command Base. Connect the Stone and Ore sites by road. Deliver Cement and Alloy to the base. Bonus: finish the roads before the first dust storm.
- **Teaches:** roads, Haulers, Stone to Cement, Ore to Alloy, dust storms.
- **Story:** The last reserve Command Base on COBALT's Carrier wakes for its terrafall. "Region claimed for the Sponsor. Arrival clock: T+31 years. Probability of contact: 0.2%."

### 2. Passenger Standard

- **Map:** Greenreach (forest). No rivals.
- **Objectives:** Turn Stalks into Fiber and Crystal into Chips, then build a Bot Foundry. Produce new Workers and a Freight Truck. Place three Outposts.
- **Teaches:** the full production chain, Bio Farms and Fuel, the Bot Foundry, Outposts.
- **Story:** The colony finds a town the robots built during the Accord years: empty houses, lit welcome signs, a bus shelter. The first murmur is logged as an acoustic anomaly.

### 3. The Ledger

- **Map:** The Long Grass (savanna). Rival: a VERMILION colony run by the Salvager (Raider).
- **Objectives:** The map has no Crystal, so trade for it at a Trade Post. Meet the *Wren* when it lands. Get convoys through the Salvager's raids with Warbot escorts.
- **Teaches:** Trade Posts, the trader ship, Warbots, convoy defense.
- **Story:** The *Wren* arrives on schedule with a 31-year-old cargo list. The Salvager: "Nice convoy. Shame about the escort."

### 4. Arrival in One Day

- **Map:** Emberwood (autumn forest). Rival: a VIRIDIAN colony run by the Herald (Rusher).
- **Objectives:** Survive the early attacks. Capture the Herald's Command Base.
- **Teaches:** Bulwarks, capturing a Command Base (everything it built becomes yours).
- **Story:** The Herald's clock has read "Arrival in one day" for 31 years. Its captured robots report for duty without a pause.

### 5. Ringfall

- **Map:** Cinderlands (volcanic ash). Rivals: the Salvager returns, joined by a VIRIDIAN colony run by the Steward (Balanced).
- **Objectives:** Race to claim the strike sites after each meteor shower. Hold the most land when the timer ends.
- **Teaches:** meteor showers, Torches, the land-majority win.
- **Story:** After the last ringfall, the road lights die one by one and a Hauler stops reporting. The first sighting of the Murmur ends Act I.

## Act II: Contingency

The war turns serious, and the colony reaches for home. Every answer it gets is silence.

### 6. Day 12,054

- **Map:** The White (polar snow). Rival: a TYRIAN colony run by the Warden (Turtle).
- **Objectives:** Break through the Warden's walls and shields. Capture its Command Base.
- **Teaches:** Howitzers, siege tactics.
- **Story:** "Position held. Day 12,054." On defeat: "Relief has not arrived." Its archive dates the last transmission. Probability of contact: 0.15%.

### 7. The Delta

- **Map:** The Sump (swamp and river delta). Rival: the Steward.
- **Objectives:** Build ports, ships and ferries. Move Fuel across the delta to keep a forward base running. Take the Steward's port on the main channel.
- **Teaches:** ports, ships, ferries, oil wells and refineries.
- **Story:** The Steward logs every attack "for review by the Accord tribunal," a body that has not met in decades.

### 8. Listening on All Channels

- **Map:** The Shoals (island chain). Rivals: the Salvager and the Steward.
- **Objectives:** Take three Uplink towers and hold them through the transmission window.
- **Teaches:** the Uplink win, naval logistics under fire.
- **Story:** The colony transmits home for the first time. "Uplink secured. Listening on all channels. No response." Probability of contact: 0.1%.

### 9. Floaters

- **Map:** Sky islands over the Rifts. Rival: a TYRIAN colony run by the Architect (Tycoon), introduced here.
- **Objectives:** Set up cargo planes to haul Crystal off the Floaters. Deliver more Chips than the Architect before the deadline, or break its air bridge with Strike Bombers.
- **Teaches:** cargo planes, Strike Bombers.
- **Story:** The Architect has stopped waiting and is building a city for itself. "I've paved more road this year than your Sponsor did in a century."

### 10. Argos

- **Map:** The moon. Rival: the Architect's lunar colony.
- **Objectives:** Land on the moon. Capture the Pathfinder's lunar relay and recover its archive.
- **Teaches:** the moon's rules (no Murmur; the rest is your call).
- **Story:** On the only silent ground in the system, the archive shows the arks' construction updates stopped years before the "on schedule" report. The Sponsors lied. Probability of contact: 0.01%. End of Act II.

## Act III: The Silence

The colony learns the truth, the Murmur rise from what the war left behind, and the last fight is over a signal no one will answer.

### 11. What the War Feeds

- **Map:** The Bloom. Rivals: none at first; a Herald colony arrives late.
- **Objectives:** Build a Research Lab. Survive the Murmur's night raids while the Lab studies them.
- **Teaches:** the Research Lab, defending against Murmur nests.
- **Story:** The Lab finds battlefields drained of every charge, robot parts laid out in rows, and a murmur that repeats the Uplink signal. Across the planet, Uplinks start going dark: the Murmur are feeding on the buried trunk lines.

### 12. Urban Renewal

- **Map:** The Architect's city, grown out of the empty town from mission 2. Rival: the Architect.
- **Objectives:** Capture the Architect's Command Base before its missile countdown ends. Bonus: enrich Uranium and build missiles of your own.
- **Teaches:** Uranium enrichment, nuclear missiles.
- **Story:** "Beginning urban renewal." The same weapons that ended Earth. Capturing the base hands you a city built for no one.

### 13. The Breach

- **Map:** The Hollow, outer caverns. Rival: the Murmur.
- **Objectives:** Land beneath a breach. Mine the deep Crystal, cutting Bloom veins for Fiber. Survive tremors and raids long enough to reach the Pathfinder's buried trunk line.
- **Teaches:** the Hollow's rules: breaches, floating Crystal, underground lakes.
- **Story:** Spiral pillars and hoards of robot parts. The Murmur's tunnels follow the trunk line; they have been feeding on the signal for decades.

### 14. The Deep

- **Map:** The Hollow, central chamber. Rival: the Murmur at full strength.
- **Objectives:** Push through to the cathedral chamber. Destroy the Deep's mound to free the trunk line.
- **Teaches:** nothing new; everything at once.
- **Story:** The Deep is never seen whole, only its tendril star rising from the mound. When the mound falls, the murmur stops for the first time. Hours later it starts again, fainter, somewhere else.

### 15. No Further Instructions

- **Map:** The Rust, around the dormant hull of the Pathfinder, where the first landings were. Rivals: what is left of the Architect, the Salvager and a last Herald.
- **Objectives:** Hold every Uplink around the Pathfinder through the full transmission and listening window.
- **Story:** The colony sends its report: "Ithaca I prepared." It listens. "Home signal analysis complete. No further instructions will follow." A pause. "Continuing current directive." The credits roll over the colony's roads, lit at night for freight only.
- **Post-credits hook:** among the machine traffic from other Catalog worlds, one signal matches no robot protocol.
