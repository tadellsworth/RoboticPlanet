Botfall · Field Manual

Land. Build. Expand.

Your robots have touched down on a dusty alien world. This manual covers everything you need to run the colony: what each building does, how goods flow, how batteries and terrain shape your plans, and how to beat the rival.

The goal

Gather raw resources, turn them into better goods, build more robots and spread across the planet. Pick a mode before each game.

Versus

A rival colony lands at the far end of the map. Capture its Lander to win. If it captures yours, you lose. A game runs about 15–30 minutes, longer on hilly maps.

Colony

No rival and no clock. Build supply chains and spread out. Milestones pop up at 10, 25, 40, 60 and 80% of the land your bots can reach.

Your first ten minutes

Tap Build, pick a building and tap a highlighted tile. Each card shows how many of that building you already have. Check the preview, then tap Build here or tap the same tile again. Placing a site is free: workers carry the materials over from storage. You can have six sites going at once.

  1. HarvesterNext to the teal stalk groves. The preview says how many groves are in reach; aim for plenty.
  2. QuarryBy the grey boulders. Stone goes into almost every building.
  3. Ore Mine and Crystal TapThe mine by the dark veins with glowing orange cracks, the tap by the violet crystals.
  4. SmelterTurns Ore and Fiber into Alloy, the backbone of everything advanced.
  5. Chip FabTurns Crystal and Alloy into Chips. Every new bot needs one.
  6. Bot FoundryBuilds the Workers and Warbots you order. Tap it and press +1 or +5. More workers means faster everything, so order a few early.
  7. Charging StationOnce you're working far from your Lander, so bots don't walk all the way home to recharge.
  8. OutpostNear your border to claim more land. Then pave Paths (1 Stone paves 4 tiles) between your busiest buildings.

Every building needs a clear tile on all sides, so leave gaps. Tap one of your buildings to set its priority, pause it or demolish it. Demolishing returns half the materials; cancelling a site returns everything delivered.

Workers and logistics

Workers do three jobs: they run buildings (one inside each production building), carry goods, and build construction sites. The top bar shows how many are free for carrying and building.

  • Keep workers free. The game always holds back about one in five for hauling. If raids or new buildings leave too few, it takes a worker off the least useful job and tells you. The fix is more workers from the Bot Foundry.
  • Priority. Tap a building or site and choose Low, Normal or High. High-priority ones get workers, builders and deliveries first. Low ones are served last and lose their worker first when you run short. A badge on the map shows anything you've changed.
  • Pause a building to free its worker until you resume it.
  • Storage. Tap your Lander or a Depot. Fill first makes workers bring finished goods there before any other storage, even a closer one. Empty out takes nothing new and moves what it holds elsewhere, which is handy before demolishing it. Tap individual goods to stop that building keeping them.
  • Paths make bots move twice as fast, and bots plan their routes to use them even when that means a short detour. Tap Paths, then drag across open ground inside your border. They're cheap: 1 Stone from storage paves 4 tiles, and tapping a path removes it and gives the Stone back. They work on slopes too, so pave your busiest routes and the passes between levels.

Batteries

Every bot runs on a battery that drains as it walks, works and fights. Bots recharge inside your Lander (three at full speed) or a Charging Station (four). Warbots also recharge inside their garrison. When more bots crowd in than a charger has slots, they share the power and charge slower.

If workers far from the Lander keep running flat, or buildings say Worker is recharging, build a Charging Station near that cluster. Workers running a building step out to recharge and come straight back.

Terrain and territory

The planet has terraces at different heights: lighter ground is higher, and dark rock walls are cliffs. Bots can't climb cliffs. They use slopes (the striped ramps) at passes, so some routes wind a long way round.

  • Ramp: build one at the foot or top of a one-level cliff to open your own route up. A pass is often worth a long paved Path too.
  • You can't place a building where it would block the only way through a pass, and resources across a cliff with no route don't count as "in reach".
  • Water and rocky crags can't be crossed at all.
  • Territory: you can only build inside your border (the dashed line). The Lander claims 8 tiles around it and each Outpost 6. If you lose an Outpost, its land goes too, and your non-military buildings left outside your border are destroyed. Gatherers only harvest on your land or unclaimed land.

War

Versus

Warbots come from the Bot Foundry and wait inside your Lander and Outposts, filling front-line Outposts first. When enemy warbots come close, a garrison sends defenders out automatically. Buildings regain armor once they've gone about 8 seconds without being hit.

Attack a building

Tap Army, then a rival building, choose how many warbots and tap Send warbots. They come from garrisons within 14 tiles of the target, and each garrison keeps one at home. Ordinary buildings are wrecked. Outposts become yours, along with their land. Capture the rival's Lander to win.

Hold an area

Tap Army, then open ground. Your warbots march there and plant a rally flag. They fight enemy bots that come near and attack enemy buildings within 3 tiles of the flag. Bots with a flat battery walk to the nearest garrison to recharge, then come back. Tap the flag to send more or call them all home.

Missiles

A Munitions Works makes Warheads and a Missile Silo fires them. Tap Army, then a rival building within 18 tiles of a loaded Silo, then Fire missile. A missile wrecks ordinary buildings and knocks Outposts and the Lander down to almost no armor, so send warbots right behind it to capture them. The blast also hurts bots nearby. When the rival fires at you, a flashing red ring shows where it will land.

Ordering bots

The Bot Foundry only builds the bots you order. Tap it, then press +1 or +5 next to Workers or Warbots; Clear cancels that queue. Mixed orders take turns. With nothing queued it sits idle and leaves your Alloy and Chips in storage.

Pulse Turrets near your Lander and Outposts wear down raids, as long as they get Crystal. Missiles are the best way to deal with a rival turret line before your warbots walk in.

Saving and settings

  • The game saves itself every 20 seconds and whenever you leave. Continue on the main menu picks up where you left off. There's one save slot, so starting a new game replaces it.
  • The speed button cycles 1×, 2× and 3×. The pause menu has sound on/off, a short How to play, and Save & quit.
  • Map settings and the minimap's hidden/shown state are remembered on each device.
