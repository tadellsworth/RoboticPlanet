# The Deep surge retest

Headless computer vs. computer on the Hollow, with two Normal rivals, Active Murmur, and a 75-minute game-time cutoff. The baseline is the working branch immediately after the upstream merge. The only simulation change in the retest is the Deep's defense bursts. Colony numbers are the in-game one-based slots.

| Size | Seed | Before | After |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Medium | 11 | 31:23, Colony 2 | 31:23, Colony 2 |
| Medium | 12 | 34:22, Colony 2 | 34:22, Colony 2 |
| Medium | 25 | 13:06, Colony 2 | 13:06, Colony 2 |
| Small | 21 | 29:29, Colony 1 | 29:06, Colony 1 |
| Small | 25 | 33:19, Colony 1 | 40:26, Colony 2 |

All five finished in both runs. In the retest the Deep stayed above its first burst threshold in the three Medium matches. It reached the final tier in both Small matches.

The first defense burst still begins below 75% armor. The middle and final tiers now begin below two-thirds and one-third armor. They send up to three guards every 24 seconds and four every 16 seconds, respectively, compared to two every 32 seconds in the first tier. Live caps of 5, 8, and 12 bound the local defense. A higher tier can trigger immediately when crossed. The violet pulse and the pitch of the rumble show the escalation.
